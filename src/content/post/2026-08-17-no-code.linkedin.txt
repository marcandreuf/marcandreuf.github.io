I forgot how to code. I am not sure that is a bad thing.

Twelve months, four projects, two SaaS products shipped. I barely looked at whether the code was clean. If it worked, I kept it.

Then the first job conversation arrived: "Please complete this coding challenge."

My take: if you are not generating 120% of your code with AI, you are losing the game. The 100% is the production code. The extra 20 to 30% is the tests, and that is the part that actually matters.

And if you are hiring, you already have the problem. So put it on the table:

"This is the problem. Now, how would you solve it?"
